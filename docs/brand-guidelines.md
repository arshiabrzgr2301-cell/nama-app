# NAMA Visual Identity

## Direction
NAMA combines futuristic property technology with the trust and clarity of architectural brands. The identity is built around midnight navy, electric blue, violet, and emerald/teal accents.

## Core palette
- Midnight background: #050B18
- Deep navy surface: #0D1A2D
- Elevated surface: #11223A
- Electric blue: #27C8F4
- Violet: #8B5CF6
- Emerald teal: #18C6A3
- Primary text: #F4F8FF
- Secondary text: #A7B7CC

## Visual principles
1. Use dark navy as the dominant canvas; avoid pure black except in immersive media.
2. Use blue for primary actions, violet for spatial/AI features, and teal for success, trust, and secure states.
3. Use restrained glows and translucent-looking surfaces; preserve contrast and readability.
4. Prefer rounded cards, fine borders, generous spacing, and clear RTL hierarchy.
5. Never communicate fake processing progress. Queue states must remain explicit until the GPU worker reports real progress.
6. Keep the mobile consultant experience focused and the organization dashboard information-dense but calm.

## Product surfaces
- Public entry and authentication: brand-first, clear trust cues, minimal actions.
- Consultant dashboard: property inventory, publication status, views, and quick actions.
- Organization workspace: organization-level reporting, team and role management, subscriptions, and auditability.
- Spatial/VR experience: controls recede and media remains the focus.

## Logo
The current app mark is a code-drawn architectural N monogram with a teal/blue/violet orbit. It is a UI implementation placeholder for the approved direction; replace with a vector master once the final logo artwork is approved.
